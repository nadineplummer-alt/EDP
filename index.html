Your six weeks

Here's what the next six weeks could look like

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Settle in

    Meet your coach and your group. Learn the key movements step by step, find your starting point and get comfortable in the space. No pressure, no judgement.

  2. Weeks 3–4

    Build momentum

    Training starts to feel familiar. You'll lift a bit more, move a bit better and notice more energy day to day. Showing up becomes part of your week.

  3. Weeks 5–6

    Feel the difference

    Stronger, fitter and more confident — with a routine you can keep. We'll review your progress together and plan what comes next.

Questions

Everything you might be wondering

I haven't exercised in years. Is this really for me?

Absolutely — this programme was designed with you in mind. Most women who join are returning after a long break or are completely new to the gym. Your coach will start where you are and build from there.

I'm nervous about walking into a gym. What's it actually like?

That's one of the most common things we hear, and it's completely normal. EDP is a women-only, friendly space with small groups and a coach in every session, so you'll never be left wondering what to do. Most women tell us the nerves are gone after the first session.

What if I can't keep up with everyone else?

You won't need to. Every exercise can be made easier or harder, and your coach will adjust it to suit you. Everyone works at their own level — it's about your progress, not anyone else's.

How many sessions a week is it?

We'll talk you through the timetable on your call and help you choose session times that fit around work, family and everything else you've got going on.

Do I need to diet or cut out foods?

No. We don't do restriction or banned foods. You'll get simple, realistic guidance that fits into family life and helps you feel better day to day.

What do I need to bring?

Just comfortable clothes, trainers and a water bottle. We'll take care of the rest.

What happens after the six weeks?

At the end of the programme we'll review your progress together. Lots of women choose to continue as members so they can keep their routine going — but there's no obligation.

How much does it cost?

The full 6-Week Foundation Programme is £XX. Leave your details and we'll talk you through everything that's included, with no pressure.
