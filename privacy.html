Everyday Performance (“we”, “us”) is a women's gym based in Armagh, Northern Ireland. This policy explains how we use the personal information you give us when you enquire through this website.

What we collect

  • Your first name, mobile number and email address
  • The answers you choose on our enquiry form (for example, your preferred training time)
  • Basic information about how you found us (such as the advert you clicked)

How we use it

We use your details only to contact you about the 6-Week Foundation Programme and to help you get started. We will never sell your information.

Cookies and advertising

This website may use the Meta Pixel to measure how well our Facebook and Instagram adverts work. You can control ad personalisation in your Facebook and Instagram settings.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and for no longer than 12 months if you don't become a member.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time, or ask us to stop contacting you. Just get in touch using the details below.
